# ADR-0027: Frontend budgets — progress of the month, budget form, stop and delete

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Deciders**: Paul

## Context

- A budget is a history of entries per expense category: "300 € from January", "350 € from June", "stopped from September" (an entry without amount). One entry per category and month. `POST` adds an entry, `PUT` changes its amount (`If-Match`), `DELETE` removes a wrong one; `GET /api/budgets/progress?month=` gives limit, spent (subcategories included), remaining, ratio and over-budget for each category with a budget that month ([ADR-0019](0019-budgets-module.md)).
- To set the budget from a month, the client must know whether that month already has an entry (change it) or not (add one). The interface must tell "stop from this month" (keeps the past) from "delete" (corrects a mistake).
- A budget sits on a parent or on its subcategories, never on both; a category with any entry counts as budgeted (`BudgetPolicy`). Otherwise the API answers `409 budget.branch_conflict`.
- Budgets have no currency: their spending adds up expenses of every currency (limit of the MVP). Amounts are never added up in the app ([ADR-0024](0024-frontend-shared-components.md)).
- Without `month`, the API uses its own month in UTC.
- The dashboard (block 8) shows the budgets of the month too; features cannot import each other ([ADR-0021](0021-frontend-foundation.md)). Roadmap: charts over time, recurring transactions and CSV import (more expenses counted), PWA, multi-user, more currencies.
- The theme has one palette (`azure`): its tertiary role is the same blue as primary.

## Decision

1. **The budgets page is the month and one row per budgeted category** (`/budgets?month=…`, the month in the URL and always sent to the API). The row opens the expenses of that category in that month (`/transactions?categoryId=…&month=…`); its menu holds "Modifica" and "Ferma da <mese>". No total: amounts of the budgets are not added up.
2. **The progress is a bar with three states** (`<app-budget-progress>` in `shared/ui`): "120,00 € di 350,00 €", then "Restano …" or "Superato di …". Normal up to 90% of the limit (`BUDGET_WARNING_RATIO`), warning from 90%, red above the limit. The warning has its own color, `--pf-warning` (and `--pf-warning-container` for the track), from an amber palette generated by Material's `theme-color` schematic like the income green: the tertiary role was invisible, and orange was too close to the error red on dark surfaces. The component takes the API's figures and computes nothing.
3. **A budget form page per category** (`/budgets/new?month=…`, `/budgets/:categoryId?month=…`): limit, "Dal mese" (the month the budgets page showed) and, below, the history of the entries. Saving reads the category's entries from the API, then changes that month's entry or adds one (`BudgetsStore.setFrom`), so the version sent is the current one. The limit in force that month is the starting value.
4. **Stop and delete as archive and delete** ([ADR-0025](0025-frontend-accounts-and-categories.md)): "Ferma da <mese>" does not ask; the snackbar offers "Annulla", which deletes the stop entry it added or puts back the amount of the entry it changed. "Elimina" on an entry of the history asks first and explains that it corrects a mistake.
5. **Categories that cannot get a budget are disabled in the selector, with the reason** ("Ha già un budget…", "Il budget è su «Casa»", "Il budget è sulle sue sottocategorie"): `<app-category-select [disabledReason]>`. The rule repeats `BudgetPolicy`; a `409 budget.branch_conflict` is still shown on the field if the two ever differ.
6. **Budgets are shown in euros** (`BUDGET_CURRENCY` in `core`), one constant to replace when the API gives budgets a currency.
7. **One E2E test**: a budget set from the page, an expense counted (warning state and color), the movements of the category, "Ferma" and "Annulla", the history.

Every budget write tells `DataChanges` that `budgets` changed: the progress, the entries and the categories in use (`isInUse`) are read again; the progress also follows `transactions` and `categories`. Budget writes have no client id and no offline queue: they are not part of the quick entry.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- The page answers "how much is left" at a glance on a phone, and leads to the expenses behind a number.
- The budget form page is the natural place for the month-by-month chart of the roadmap.
- The same rules as accounts and categories: nothing the API would refuse is offered, reversible actions do not ask, irreversible ones do.
- Expenses from recurring transactions and CSV import are counted with no change here.
- The dashboard reuses the progress bar and its colors.

**Negative**
- The branch rule lives in two places (domain and selector).
- Budgets in other currencies would show the euro sign; spending of every currency is added by the API.
- The warning threshold (90%) is a choice of the app, not a per-user preference yet.
- "Annulla" of a stop has two cases (delete the new entry or restore the amount).
- After deleting an entry from the history, the form keeps the values typed so far: saving creates the entry again.

## Alternatives considered

**Rows that open the budget form** — Rejected: one more step to see the expenses behind a number; the form stays one tap away in the menu.

**Every expense category on the page, "Imposta" on those without a budget** — Rejected: about 20 rows where the budgets get lost.

**Two states (normal, over)** — Rejected: the person learns only after going over. **Numbers only** — Rejected: harder to read at a glance.

**Setting a budget only "from this month"** — Rejected: a mistake in a past month could not be corrected from the app.

**A dialog from the row** — Rejected: no address, the back button closes the app on phones ([ADR-0025](0025-frontend-accounts-and-categories.md)).

**A confirmation for "Ferma" too** — Rejected: a question for something "Annulla" undoes.

**Letting the API refuse the category** — Rejected: the error comes only after "Salva". It remains as a fallback.

**Amounts without a currency sign** — Rejected: harder to read and unlike the rest of the app.

**A tertiary palette in the theme** — Rejected: every Material component using tertiary would change color, and the prebuilt palettes are saturated on dark surfaces. **The error container for the warning** — Rejected: two reds for two different states.
